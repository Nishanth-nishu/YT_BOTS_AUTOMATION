# Hook library (first 5 seconds)

The host says the hook on camera, big, with the sweat drop and "!!". The job of
these five seconds: make the viewer slightly anxious that they are about to
miss something that affects their job, money or status, with a wink. Funny
first, scary second, never a lie the body cannot pay off.

Rules
- At most 18 spoken words (the rules checker enforces it), target 9 to 14. Under 5 s at `am_fenrir`, speed 1.05 (about 2.5 words/s).
- Two sentences: the jab, then the news. The jab names the viewer ("coders", "everyone learning Python", "freshers").
- Plain spoken English. Contractions welcome. No year or long number in the hook (TTS reads them slowly).
- No link, no "source", no repo name that is also a URL. The reel never gives the link; the payoff asks for the comment keyword.
- Trend hooks must be true today (check the research brief's date).

Formulas

1. Job scare
   - "Coders, be scared. DeepSeek just open-sourced your replacement."
   - "Want a job next year? Then don't skip this one."
2. Everyone-knows-but-you
   - "Everyone's already using this, and you're still stuck on ChatGPT."
3. Money / time loss
   - "This one tool saves four hours a day, and you don't even know it exists."
4. Trending-now
   - "What happened in AI today just made your resume out of date."
5. Boss / interview
   - "They'll ask this in your next interview, and you won't have an answer."
6. Mock warning (funny)
   - "Don't watch this, or you'll find out how far behind you are."
7. Number shock
   - "948 exploits. One model. One week." (numbers only when verified)
8. Confession
   - "I tried this three days ago and my whole workflow changed."

Payoff / CTA (last scene, always the same shape)
- Narration: "Want the link? Comment LETS GO and I'll send it to your DMs. And follow, so you're not the last to know."
- cta pill: "Comment LETS GO, the link lands in your DMs"
- Instagram caption last line: "Want the link? Comment LETS GO and I'll DM it to you."

Scoring a hook (1 to 5 each): anxiety (does the viewer feel they might lose something), funny (is there a wink), clarity (understood on mute from the captions), truth (the body proves it). Pick the highest total; a hook under 3 on truth is out.

# Hinglish hook library (first 5 seconds)

The host says the hook on camera, big, with the sweat drop and "!!". The job of
these five seconds: make the viewer slightly anxious that they are about to
miss something that affects their job, money or status, with a wink. Funny
first, scary second, never a lie the body cannot pay off.

Rules
- At most 18 spoken words (the rules checker enforces it), target 9 to 14. Under 5 s at `hm_omega`, speed 1.15.
- Two sentences: the jab, then the news. The jab names the viewer ("coders", "jo log Python seekh rahe hain", "freshers").
- Hindi words in Devanagari, English tech words in Latin. `captionText` is the same line romanised, same word count.
- No link, no "source", no repo name that is also a URL. The reel never gives the link; the payoff asks for the comment keyword.
- Trend hooks must be true today (check the research brief's date).

Formulas (narration / captionText)

1. Job scare
   - "Coders, डर जाओ। DeepSeek ने तुम्हारा replacement open-source कर दिया।" / "Coders, dar jao. DeepSeek ne tumhara replacement open-source kar diya."
   - "2027 में job चाहिए? तो ये reel skip मत करना।" / "2027 mein job chahiye? Toh ye reel skip mat karna."
2. Everyone-knows-but-you
   - "सब लोग ये use कर रहे हैं, और तुम अभी भी ChatGPT पर अटके हो।" / "Sab log ye use kar rahe hain, aur tum abhi bhi ChatGPT par atke ho."
3. Money / time loss
   - "ये एक tool तुम्हारे 4 घंटे रोज़ बचाएगा, और तुम्हें पता भी नहीं।" / "Ye ek tool tumhare 4 ghante roz bachayega, aur tumhe pata bhi nahi."
4. Trending-now
   - "आज AI में जो हुआ, उसके बाद तुम्हारा resume पुराना हो गया।" / "Aaj AI mein jo hua, uske baad tumhara resume purana ho gaya."
5. Boss / interview
   - "अगले interview में ये पूछेंगे, और तुम्हें जवाब नहीं आएगा।" / "Agle interview mein ye poochenge, aur tumhe jawab nahi aayega."
6. Mock warning (funny)
   - "ये reel मत देखो, वरना तुम्हें पता चल जाएगा कि तुम कितने पीछे हो।" / "Ye reel mat dekho, warna tumhe pata chal jayega ki tum kitne peeche ho."
7. Number shock
   - "948 exploits। एक model ने। एक हफ्ते में।" / "948 exploits. Ek model ne. Ek hafte mein." (numbers only when verified)
8. Confession
   - "मैंने ये 3 दिन पहले try किया और अब मेरा workflow बदल गया।" / "Maine ye 3 din pehle try kiya aur ab mera workflow badal gaya."

Payoff / CTA (last scene, always the same shape)
- Narration: "Link चाहिए? Comment में लिखो LETS GO, मैं DM में भेज दूँगा। Follow करना मत भूलना।"
- captionText: "Link chahiye? Comment mein likho LETS GO, main DM mein bhej dunga. Follow karna mat bhoolna."
- cta pill: "Comment LETS GO, link DM mein aayega"
- Instagram caption last line: "Link chahiye? Comment karo LETS GO, main DM mein bhej dunga."

Scoring a hook (1 to 5 each): anxiety (does the viewer feel they might lose something), funny (is there a wink), clarity (understood on mute from the captions), truth (the body proves it). Pick the highest total; a hook under 3 on truth is out.
